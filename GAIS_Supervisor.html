What this prototype actually does

  • Simulates independent agents submitting proposed actions to a common supervisor.
  • Applies visible rules to flag possible risks, missing verification and sensitive actions.
  • Creates a human review queue and records simulated decisions.
  • Exports local audit records for inspection and testing.

What real-world deployment requires

  • Authorised integration: AI providers must send action requests to GAIS before execution.
  • Enforcement gateway: The actual business system must refuse execution without a valid approval; a dashboard alone cannot stop anything.
  • Identity and security: authenticated reviewers, role-based permissions, tamper-resistant logs, encryption and data protection.
  • Validation: independent tests, false-negative analysis, sector-specific controls and incident response.
  • Optional AI reasoning: a securely hosted language model may help interpret complex proposals, but cannot replace hard permission checks.

Do not use this standalone prototype to make real clinical, financial, driving or other safety-critical decisions.
